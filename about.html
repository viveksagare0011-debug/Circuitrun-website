🎓 Built by students, for students

We got tired of losing entire afternoons for one resistor. So we fixed it.

CircuitRun started as a simple frustration shared by every engineering student in Nerul, Vashi, and Belapur: your mini-project needs one small component, and getting it means an entire afternoon lost to travel — buses, trains, and a shop that might not even have what you need when you get there.

We're a small team of engineering students ourselves, building this alongside our own coursework, projects, and exams. This isn't a big company — it's a handful of us trying to solve a problem we deal with every semester, for everyone around us dealing with the same thing.

What we do

We collect component requests from students across campus, source everything in organized batches, and deliver straight to your hostel, department, or wherever's easiest — no travel required on your end. Need something specific for a project? We'll quote you a price before you pay anything.
