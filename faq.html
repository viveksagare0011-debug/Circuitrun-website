Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How does payment work?

You pay an advance (about 20% of your order) by UPI to confirm it. The rest is paid in cash when your order is delivered. The exact amounts are shown at checkout before you confirm.

When will my order be delivered?

We deliver in batch runs on Tuesdays and Fridays. At checkout you see the exact run your order is assigned to. An order needs to be placed at least 2 days before a run, otherwise it goes to the next one. A run can occasionally be delayed by a day, and we tell you on WhatsApp if that happens.

Which campuses do you deliver to?

We serve campuses in Nerul, Vashi and Belapur, Navi Mumbai. You choose the delivery spot on campus at checkout, for example your hostel or department.

Do I need to create an account?

There is no password. The first time you order, you enter your name, WhatsApp number, year, college and department. We use these to identify you and to contact you about your order.

How do I track my order?

Open My Orders on the site (the orders button at the top). You can see the status of each order there: Confirmed, Packed, Out for Delivery or Delivered. You can also open your invoice from there.

What if I receive a wrong, damaged or defective item?

You can ask for a return or replacement within 7 days of delivery. Open My Orders and use the Return or Replace button on that order, with your reason. We then contact you on WhatsApp to arrange the next steps.

Can I get my advance back?

Usually not once we have already bought your component in a batch run. If we cannot fulfil your order at all, your advance is refunded in full or adjusted against a future order, whichever you prefer. Refunds are paid manually by UPI within a few days of approval. The full rules are on the Terms & Policy page.

What if you do not list the component I need?

Use the Request a component form on the home page. Tell us what you need, and we reply with availability and a price.

Can you put together a full kit for my project?

Yes. See the Project Kits page, or send us your idea using the project request form and we will prepare a component list and a quote.

Who runs CircuitRun?

CircuitRun is run by a small team of engineering students, alongside our own studies. You can read more on the About page.

How do you use my data?

Only to run your orders and contact you about them. We do not sell or share it with advertisers. Details are in the Privacy section of the Terms & Policy page.

Still have a question?

Message us on Instagram or use the request form. We read every message ourselves.
