1. Ordering & Payment

When you place an order, you pay a partial advance online via UPI to confirm it, with the remaining balance due in cash on delivery (COD).

  • The advance confirms your order and reserves your spot in the next available batch run.
  • Prices shown at checkout are final for that order — we don't change pricing after an order is confirmed.
  • Please double-check your delivery location and contact number before confirming — these are used to deliver your order.

2. Delivery & Batch Runs

We source components in scheduled batch runs (typically twice a week) rather than promising instant delivery. When you check out, you'll see the specific date your order is assigned to. An order placed Tuesday to Thursday is delivered on the next Tuesday, and an order placed Friday to Monday on the next Friday, which gives us the time we need to source everything properly.

  • Delivery happens to the campus location you provide at checkout (hostel, department, or another on-campus point).
  • Occasionally a batch run may be delayed by a day due to availability or logistics — we'll let you know on WhatsApp if that happens.

3. Returns & Replacements

Returns and replacements are accepted within 7 days of delivery, for components that are defective, damaged, or incorrect.

To request one, open "My Orders" on the site and use the Return or Replace button on the relevant order — this sends us your request directly along with your reason. We'll follow up with you on WhatsApp to arrange the next steps.

  • The item should be unused and in the condition you received it, unless the return reason is that it arrived damaged or defective.
  • Return/replace requests are not accepted once the 7-day window from delivery has passed, except in cases of a genuine defect discovered later.

4. Refunds

Because we run on very thin margins as a student-run operation, our advance payment is generally non-refundable once a batch run has been sourced for it — this covers our cost of already purchasing the component on your behalf.

  • If we are unable to fulfil your order at all (component unavailable, sourcing issue on our end), your advance will be refunded in full or adjusted against a future order — your choice.
  • If a return/replace request is approved due to a defective or wrong item, we'll either send a replacement or refund the full amount paid for that item, depending on what you prefer.
  • Refunds, when applicable, are processed manually via UPI within a few days of approval.

5. Privacy & Your Data

We collect only what's needed to actually fulfil your order and get it to you:

  • Name, WhatsApp number, year, college, college floor or department, and pincode — to identify you and communicate about your order.
  • Delivery location and pincode — to know where on campus to bring your order, and to check that we deliver to your area.
  • Order history — so you can track past orders and we can process returns/replacements correctly.
  • Reviews you submit — your name, college, star rating and comment are shown publicly on the home page once we approve the review. Please do not put your phone number or anything private in a review.
  • Questions typed into the site assistant — saved without your name or number (digits and emails are masked) so we can see what students ask and improve the answers.

Your browser also stores a few things on your own device (your login details, your cart and your list of orders) so you do not have to type them again. Clearing your browser data removes them. Our database provider (Supabase) stores the order information described above on our behalf.

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It does not receive your name, number or order details, and it is switched off for browsers that send the Do Not Track signal.

This information is used only to run CircuitRun — to process your orders, contact you about them, and improve what we offer. We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or unrelated third parties. As a small, student-run operation, we don't currently have a fully independent, professionally audited security setup — we handle your data as carefully as we can, but please avoid sharing anything sensitive beyond what the order form asks for.

6. Contact & Disputes

Got an issue with an order, a question about these terms, or a dispute you'd like resolved? Email us at circuitrun0411@gmail.com, message us on Instagram (@circuitrun.in) or use the "Request a component" form on the site. We're a small team and read every message ourselves.
